THE ATELIER

The Art Behind Every Piece.

Our Surat atelier brings together master karigars, generations of Gujarati technique, and an unhurried respect for the material. No machine can imitate the hand.

01

DESIGN

Every jewel begins as a drawing — Gujarati motifs, temple geometry and the client's own story, composed on paper before gold is touched.

02

CRAFT

Karigars shape, cast, set and engrave entirely by hand. A bridal set alone can pass through two hundred hours of skilled work.

03

FINISH

Final polish, BIS hallmarking, and a last inspection beneath the lamp — only then does a piece earn the Mangalam name.

A Mangalam jewel is finished only when it feels inevitable — as if it had always existed, waiting for you.
