Since 1962

A family atelier in the heart of Surat

Mangalam Jewellers began in 1962 as a small goldsmith's workshop in Surat. Three generations later, the same families of karigars still shape every jewel by hand — now for the brides, daughters and grandchildren of our first customers.

Every commission begins with a conversation — about the wedding, the silks, the moments that will be treasured. Only then do our designers begin sketching, composing each piece as part of a story.

MangalbhaiFounder, 1962

“We believe the most beautiful jewellery isn't remembered for its price. It's remembered for the moment it became yours.”

Our journey

Milestones of a family house

1962

The first workshop

Mangalbhai opens a two-bench goldsmith's atelier in Surat's old city.

1989

The hallmark promise

Mangalam becomes one of Gujarat's first BIS-hallmarked family jewellers.

2026

Heritage, renewed

The third generation brings Gujarati craft to jewellery designed for modern life.
