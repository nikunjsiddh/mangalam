Since 1962

A family atelier in the heart of Surat

Mangalam Jewellers began in 1962 as a small goldsmith's workshop in Surat. Three generations later, the same families of karigars still shape every jewel by hand — now for the brides, daughters and grandchildren of our first customers.

Every commission begins with a conversation — about the wedding, the silks, the moments that will be treasured. Only then do our designers begin sketching, composing each piece as part of a story.
